Research

Satellite archives arrive as thousands of separate files in different shapes. My work makes them one queryable structure, so scientists can slice, aggregate and compare storms without writing new preprocessing scripts each time.

PrecipCube

Manuscript in preparation

A multi-satellite datacube platform that lets a geostationary imager and a space-borne radar be explored side by side, in one web interface.

~3.6 TBINSAT-3DR and GPM-DPR data
2020–2025Monsoon seasons over India
2 sensorsMatched through one index
  • Each archive stays in its native layout and is registered with a configuration file, so new sensors plug in without rewriting the platform.
  • A lightweight matched-observation index collocates passive and active sensor readings and fetches the matching measurements on demand.
  • Slicing, dicing, roll-up, drill-down and drill-across queries, event detection, cross-sections and export, all from the browser.

GPMKUBE

IEEE IGARSS 2026 · oral

A visual-analytical framework for the GPM Dual-frequency Precipitation Radar, built to look inside storms layer by layer.

~3 TBOrbit-based HDF5 swaths processed
~23%Smaller on disk as chunked Zarr
3DVertical precipitation profiles
  • Converted Level-2 Ku-band radar swaths into optimized multidimensional Zarr datasets.
  • OLAP operations for dicing, roll-up, cross-section extraction and threshold-based event detection.
  • Interactive views of radar reflectivity and storm structure, tuned with chunking strategies for fast queries.

A scalable, analysis-ready datacube for India's INSAT-3DR geostationary satellite.

  • Turned high-frequency INSAT HDF5 imagery into multidimensional HDF5 and cloud-optimized Zarr cubes.
  • Built preprocessing for calibration, coordinate transformation and analysis-ready products.
  • Slicing, dicing, roll-up and extreme-event analysis for meteorological studies, and ML/DL rainfall nowcasting from INSAT-3DR bands.
